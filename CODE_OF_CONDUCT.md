# Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, respectful and technically rigorous community.

- Be constructive and courteous; criticize ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy. Do not post credentials, private conversations, account identifiers or raw authenticated captures.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or dox anyone.
- Report security concerns privately where possible rather than publishing exploit details.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect contributors. Enforcement decisions are made in good faith. This is a short project-specific policy, not a claim of adopting another organization's formal enforcement process.
